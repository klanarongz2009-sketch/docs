## Getting started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Go to [zap-games-architect.vercel.app](https://zap-games-architect.vercel.app) and authenticate with Google, Apple, or legacy credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the game catalog">
    View available simulations and their current status on the dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the simulation">
    Select an active game and click **AUTHENTICATE** to begin your session.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track your progress">
    Your scores and session history are saved automatically to your player profile.
  </Step>
</Steps>
