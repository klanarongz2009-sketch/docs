## What is Zap Games?

Zap Games hosts a growing catalog of simulation games set in a dystopian cyberpunk universe. Each game is an immersive, standalone experience with its own mechanics, lore, and challenges.

### Key features

- **Cyberpunk simulation games** — Enter dark, atmospheric worlds with deep narrative and fast-paced mechanics.
- **Flexible authentication** — Sign in with your Google or Apple account, or use email credentials. All sessions are encrypted via Firebase Auth (TLS 1.3).
- **Cross-device access** — Play from any browser-connected device without installing anything.
- **Progress tracking** — Your achievements, unlocks, and session history are saved automatically.
- **Simulation status system** — Know at a glance whether a game is live and accepting players.
- **Architect-verified games** — Community-built simulations reviewed and verified by the Zap Games Architect.

## Getting started

New to Zap Games? Start here:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Visit [zap-games-architect.vercel.app](https://zap-games-architect.vercel.app) and sign up using Google, Apple, or a username and password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Use your chosen credentials to authenticate and access the platform dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse the game catalog">
    View available simulations and their current status on the main screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch a game">
    Select an active simulation and click **AUTHENTICATE** to enter the game world.
  </Step>
</Steps>
