Find answers to the most common questions about Zap Games below. If you don't find what you need here, visit the [troubleshooting guide](/help/troubleshooting) for step-by-step help with specific issues.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is Zap Games?">
    Zap Games is a browser-based cyberpunk gaming platform hosting immersive simulation games. You play entirely in the browser — no downloads or installations required.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I create an account?">
    Your account is created automatically when you first sign in. Visit [zap-games-architect.vercel.app](https://zap-games-architect.vercel.app) and sign in using Google, Apple, or a username and password. No separate registration form is required.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What does DORMANT mean?">
    **DORMANT** means the game is temporarily offline and not currently accepting players. This status is normal and temporary — Zap Games reactivates simulations on a rolling schedule. Refresh the page or check back after some time. Games marked **LIVE** are currently active (e.g. NODE\_DEF).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why can't I access any games?">
    If all games show DORMANT, they are temporarily offline. This happens during maintenance or scheduled rotation. Note that **NODE\_DEF** is currently LIVE. If you still can't access it, see the [troubleshooting guide](/help/troubleshooting).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Which sign-in methods are supported?">
    Zap Games supports three methods:

    - **Google** — Sign in with your Google account.
    - **Apple** — Sign in with your Apple ID.
    - **Email** — Use an email and password directly within Zap Games.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I switch my sign-in method after signing up?">
    No. Your sign-in method is linked at account creation and cannot be changed afterward. If you need to switch providers, contact support for assistance.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is my game progress saved?">
    Yes. All scores, session history, and achievements are automatically saved to your player profile. You can view your full progress history at any time from your dashboard.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I reset my game stats?">
    Yes. Go to **Settings \> My Progress** and select **Reset Stats** for the game you want to reset. Note that this is permanent — deleted stats cannot be recovered.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are the games free to play?">
    Access to the Zap Games platform and its simulations is included with your account. Visit the platform for the most up-to-date information on any premium features or upcoming offerings.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Zap Games work on mobile?">
    Zap Games is designed for browser play. While the platform is accessible on mobile browsers, gameplay is optimized for desktop. NODE\_DEF uses mouse click controls, making it more mobile-friendly than keyboard-based games like Neon Void Rider and Neural Breach.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What does the 'System Under Development' warning mean?">
    This warning appears during active maintenance or optimization work on the authentication system. The platform remains accessible for authorized users. If you encounter this message and cannot sign in, wait a short time and try again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I delete my account?">
    Contact Zap Games support to request account deletion. This is a permanent, irreversible action — all your data, scores, and history will be removed.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
