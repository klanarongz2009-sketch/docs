This guide covers the most common issues players encounter on Zap Games and how to resolve them. If you're experiencing a problem, find your issue below and follow the steps.

## Sign-in problems

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I can't sign in with Google">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Check pop-up permissions">
        Google sign-in uses a pop-up window. Ensure your browser isn't blocking pop-ups from the Zap Games domain. In most browsers, look for a blocked pop-up indicator in the address bar and allow it.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Confirm your Google account">
        Make sure you're signed into the correct Google account in your browser before clicking the Google sign-in button.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Clear cookies and retry">
        Clear your browser cookies for the Zap Games domain and try signing in again.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Try a different browser">
        If the issue persists, try signing in from a different browser (e.g., Chrome, Firefox, Safari).
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I can't sign in with Apple">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Check your Apple ID status">
        Ensure your Apple ID is active and in good standing at appleid.apple.com.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Allow pop-ups">
        Apple Sign-In may open a pop-up. Check that your browser allows pop-ups from the Zap Games domain.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Try on Safari">
        Apple Sign-In works most reliably in Safari. If you're on another browser, try Safari as a fallback.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I forgot my email password">
    On the Zap Games login screen, select **Continue with Email**, then click the password reset link. You'll receive a reset email at the address associated with your account. Follow the link in the email to set a new password.

    <Note>
      Password reset emails can sometimes land in your spam folder. Check there if you don't see the email within a few minutes.
    </Note>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My login keeps failing with no error message">
    Try the following in order:

    1. Clear your browser cache and cookies.
    2. Disable any browser extensions (especially ad blockers or privacy tools) that might interfere with the login flow.
    3. Try a private/incognito browser window.
    4. Try a different browser entirely.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Game access problems

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="All games show DORMANT">
    This is expected behavior when all simulations are temporarily offline. Zap Games rotates and reactivates simulations on a schedule. Note that **NODE\_DEF** is currently LIVE — if you still cannot access it, wait a few minutes and refresh. If games have been dormant for an unusually long time, the platform may be undergoing extended maintenance.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A game shows ACTIVE but won't load">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Hard refresh the page">
        Press `Ctrl+Shift+R` (Windows/Linux) or `Cmd+Shift+R` (Mac) to force a full page reload.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Clear your browser cache">
        Go to your browser settings and clear cached files for the Zap Games domain.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Check your connection">
        Make sure you have a stable internet connection. Game simulations require a consistent connection to load correctly.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Try another browser">
        If the game loads in a different browser, your primary browser may have a compatibility issue. Report this via the feedback option in your account settings.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The game loads but performs poorly">
    Poor performance is usually caused by:

    - **Slow internet connection** — Zap Games simulations require a stable broadband connection.
    - **Too many browser tabs** — Close unused tabs to free up memory.
    - **Browser extensions** — Disable extensions and try again.
    - **Outdated browser** — Update your browser to the latest version for best performance.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Account problems

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My progress isn't saving">
    Progress saves automatically at the end of each session. If you exited a game abruptly (e.g., closed the tab mid-session), that session's data may not have been recorded. Ensure you reach the natural end of a game run before closing the browser.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My display name change isn't showing up">
    Display name changes take effect immediately but may take a few seconds to propagate across leaderboards. Refresh the leaderboard page after making a change.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I can't find my account">
    If you can't find your account, you may have signed up with a different provider than you're currently using. Try each sign-in method (Google, Apple, Email) to locate your original account.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Still having issues?

If none of the above steps resolve your problem, you can:

- Check the [FAQ](/help/faq) for additional answers.
- Reach out to Zap Games support through the **Help** option in your account dashboard.

<Tip>
  When contacting support, include your display name, the sign-in method you use, and a description of the issue (including any error messages). This helps the support team resolve your issue faster.
</Tip>
