Neon Void Rider is a fast-paced arcade survival game set in a luminous cyberpunk void. Your goal is simple: ride as far as you can through a neon-drenched gauntlet while avoiding hazards that grow faster and more complex over time. Every run is unique, and every second counts.

Once Neon Void Rider is **ACTIVE**, here's how to enter and play:

<Steps>

## Gameplay mechanics

Neon Void Rider is built around reflex and endurance:

- **Obstacle avoidance** — The void is filled with geometric hazards that increase in speed and density as your run progresses.
- **Score multipliers** — Chain near-misses to build multipliers and climb the leaderboard.
- **Session continuity** — Your best run scores are saved automatically to your player profile.

## Controls

| Action | Control |
| --- | --- |
| Move left | `←` Arrow or `A` |
| Move right | `→` Arrow or `D` |
| Brake / slow | `↓` Arrow or `S` |
| Boost | `Space` |
| Pause | `Esc` |

## Tips for new players

<Tip>
  Start slow. The void accelerates naturally — let the game's rhythm guide your movement before pushing for high scores.
</Tip>

- **Watch the edges** — The void boundaries are your enemy early on. Stay centered.
- **Don't chase score** — Survival time correlates more strongly with final score than aggressive play.
- **Use the brake sparingly** — Slowing down can help in dense sections but costs momentum when you need it most.
