Neural Breach puts you inside a cyberpunk hacking simulation where you must infiltrate increasingly hardened network nodes without triggering security countermeasures. Patience, observation, and timing are your primary tools. One misstep can collapse your entire operation.

## How to play

Once Neural Breach is **ACTIVE**, here's how to enter and start your operation:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your dashboard">
    Sign in to Zap Games and navigate to your main dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Neural Breach">
    Click **Neural Breach** from the game list. Confirm the simulation is showing an **ACTIVE** status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Initiate the breach">
    Click **AUTHENTICATE** to enter the simulation. The hacking interface will load in your browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your entry point">
    Each session presents a network map. Analyze the security layers and select your initial entry node.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the breach">
    Navigate through the network without triggering alarms. Reach the target node to complete the mission.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Core mechanics

Neural Breach revolves around stealth and decision-making:

- **Network maps** — Each level presents a procedurally arranged web of nodes. Some are guarded; others are open.
- **Security layers** — Nodes have varying levels of encryption. Bypassing higher-security nodes takes longer and carries greater detection risk.
- **Detection meter** — Every action slightly raises your detection level. If it hits maximum, countermeasures activate and your session ends.
- **Time pressure** — Security protocols tighten over time, making later moves harder.

## Strategy tips

<Tip>
  Map your full route before moving. Changing course mid-breach raises your detection level significantly.
</Tip>

- **Prioritize low-security paths** — Even if longer, they keep your detection level low.
- **Watch timing patterns** — Security sweeps follow predictable intervals. Move during gaps.
- **Don't rush** — The detection meter rises from actions, not time. A methodical approach almost always beats speed.

<Warning>
  If your detection meter hits maximum, your session ends immediately and any progress for that run is lost. Some high-value target nodes trigger faster countermeasures — plan accordingly.
</Warning>
