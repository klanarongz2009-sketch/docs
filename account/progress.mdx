Zap Games automatically tracks your performance across all simulation games. Your progress page gives you a complete record of every session, your personal best scores, and where you rank among other players.

## Accessing your progress

From the Zap Games dashboard, click your avatar and select **My Progress**. You'll see a summary of your activity across all games.

## What gets tracked

For each game you play, Zap Games records:

- **Total sessions played** — How many times you've entered the simulation.
- **Best score / best run** — Your top performance for that game.
- **Average performance** — Your typical score or survival time across all runs.
- **Last played** — When you most recently completed a session.
- **Achievements unlocked** — Milestones you've hit within each game.

## NODE\_DEF stats

Your NODE\_DEF progress includes:

| Metric | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Packets intercepted | Total packets blocked across all sessions |
| Core Integrity best | Highest Core Integrity % maintained at session end |
| Total sessions | Total number of play sessions |
| Longest survival | Your record survival time against threats |

## Neon Void Rider stats

Your Neon Void Rider progress includes:

| Metric | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Best distance | Furthest you've traveled in a single run |
| Top score | Highest score across all sessions |
| Total runs | Total number of play sessions |
| Longest survival | Your record survival time |

## Neural Breach stats

Your Neural Breach progress includes:

| Metric | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Missions completed | Total successful network breaches |
| Best time | Fastest mission completion |
| Success rate | Percentage of runs completed without detection |
| Highest-security node breached | Most challenging target you've cleared |

## Leaderboards

Zap Games maintains global leaderboards for each game. Your ranking is based on your best score and is updated in real time after each session.

<Note>
  Leaderboard positions are only visible if your profile is set to **Public**. Change this in [account settings](/account/settings).
</Note>

## Progress reset

If you want to reset your stats for a specific game, go to **Settings \> My Progress** and select **Reset Stats** for the relevant game. This permanently removes your scores and session history for that title.

<Warning>
  Progress resets are permanent. Deleted stats cannot be recovered.
</Warning>
