Zap Games account settings let you control how the platform looks and behaves for you. Access your settings from the dashboard by clicking your avatar and selecting **Settings**.

## Display preferences

Customize the look and feel of the Zap Games interface:

| Setting | Options | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Color scheme | Dark / System | Dark |
| Interface language | English | English |
| Animation effects | On / Off | On |

<Note>
  Zap Games is designed around a dark, terminal-style aesthetic. The dark color scheme is recommended for the best visual experience.
</Note>

## Notification settings

Control which notifications Zap Games sends to you:

- **Game status updates** — Get notified when a game transitions from DORMANT to ACTIVE.
- **Session summaries** — Receive a recap after each game session, including your score and rank.
- **Platform announcements** — Hear about new games, features, and scheduled maintenance windows.

To toggle notifications, go to **Settings \> Notifications** and switch each category on or off.

## Privacy controls

Manage how your data and activity are visible:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Leaderboard visibility">
    By default, your display name and scores appear on public leaderboards. You can set your profile to **Private** in Settings to hide your scores from other players. You will still be ranked internally but won't appear on public lists.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Play history">
    Your session history is private by default. Only you can view the games you've played and your performance data.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Password management (email accounts only)

If you signed up with email credentials, you can update your password at any time:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Settings">
    Click your avatar and select **Settings** from the dropdown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Security">
    Navigate to the **Security** tab within Settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change your password">
    Enter your current password, then enter and confirm your new password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Update Password**. You'll be prompted to sign in again with your new credentials.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If you signed up using Google or Apple, password management is handled by that provider. Update your password through your Google or Apple account settings.
</Note>
