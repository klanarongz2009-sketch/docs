Your Zap Games player profile is your identity on the platform. It stores your display name, linked sign-in providers, and gameplay history. You can view and update your profile at any time from your account settings.

## Viewing your profile

After signing in, access your profile by clicking your avatar or username in the top corner of the dashboard. Your profile page shows:

- **Display name** — The name other players see on leaderboards.
- **Sign-in method** — Whether you're using Google, Apple, or email credentials.
- **Account creation date** — When you first joined Zap Games.
- **Total play sessions** — A count of all game sessions you've started.

## Updating your display name

Your display name appears on leaderboards and in game sessions. To change it:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your profile">
    Click your avatar or username in the dashboard to go to your profile page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Edit">
    Select **Edit Profile** next to your display name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a new name">
    Type your preferred display name. Names must be between 3 and 20 characters and contain no special characters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Changes**. Your new name takes effect immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Linked sign-in providers

Your profile shows which sign-in method you use. If you signed up with Google or Apple, your account is linked to that provider. Legacy credential accounts are managed directly within Zap Games.

<Note>
  You cannot switch between sign-in providers (e.g., from Google to Apple) after account creation. If you need to change providers, contact support.
</Note>

## Account deletion

To permanently delete your account and all associated data, contact Zap Games support. Account deletion is irreversible — all progress, scores, and history will be removed.

<Warning>
  Account deletion cannot be undone. Make sure you want to permanently remove your profile before submitting a request.
</Warning>
