Zap Games gets you into a cyberpunk simulation as quickly as possible. This guide walks you through creating an account, signing in, and launching your first game — from zero to playing in under five minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Zap Games">
    Open [zap-games-architect.vercel.app](https://zap-games-architect.vercel.app) in your browser. No installation required — Zap Games runs entirely in the browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    On the login screen, choose one of the following sign-in options:

    - **Google** — Sign in with your Google account for instant access.
    - **Apple** — Use your Apple ID for quick, secure sign-in.
    - **Email** — Enter your email and password if you prefer not to use a third-party provider.

    If this is your first visit, your account is created automatically when you sign in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate">
    Click the **AUTHENTICATE** button after entering your credentials. You'll be taken to the main platform dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse available simulations">
    The dashboard displays the current game catalog. Each game shows its title and current simulation status:

    - **LIVE** — The game is running and ready to play. (e.g. NODE\_DEF)
    - **DORMANT** — The game is temporarily offline. Check back later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the simulation">
    Select an active game and confirm entry. You'll be dropped directly into the game world.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What to expect

When you first arrive at Zap Games, you'll see a terminal-style interface displaying available games and their statuses. The platform is intentionally minimal — all focus goes to the game experience itself.

<Note>
  If a game shows **DORMANT**, it is temporarily offline. Zap Games reactivates simulations regularly — refresh the page or check back in a few hours. **NODE\_DEF** is currently **LIVE** and ready to play.
</Note>
